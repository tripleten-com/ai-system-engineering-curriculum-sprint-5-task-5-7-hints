# Step 4: Reconstruct your recovery run from its trail alone

## You already know what the run will do

<details><summary>Hint 1</summary>

Is this the recovery script you ran in Step 1? If you know its steps and tokens already, the reconstruction tests your memory, not the trail.

</details>

## You can't find who approved

<details><summary>Hint 1</summary>

Which record names the decider? Which field in it is the dispatcher's identity?

</details>

<details><summary>Hint 2</summary>

The role isn't in the record. What does the approval point guarantee about the role of every decision it accepts?

</details>

## The total tokens don't match

<details><summary>Hint 1</summary>

Which record did you take the total from: one proposal's record, or the record that ends the run?

</details>

## A value disagrees with the saved output

<details><summary>Hint 1</summary>

Is the trail missing an event, or is an event carrying the wrong value? Find the record you'd have needed and check where in the code it's written.

</details>
